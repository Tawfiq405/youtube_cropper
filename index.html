🎬 YouTube Video Cropper

📋 How to Use:

  • Paste YouTube URL: Copy any YouTube video URL and paste it in the input field above
  • ✂️ Draw Crop: Click to activate crop mode, then draw a selection box on the video. Click again to apply the crop
  • ⛶ Fullscreen: Enter fullscreen mode to view your cropped video (YouTube's controls remain accessible)
  • Zoom: Use slider to zoom in and crop the video (1x = original, 3x = maximum zoom)
  • Position: Move the video horizontally and vertically when zoomed
  • Width/Height: Adjust the container size
  • Aspect Ratio Presets: Quick buttons to change the viewing format
  • Reset: Return all settings to default values
